The Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) requires institutions to hold enough high-quality liquid assets to cover total net cash outflows over a 30-day stress period. The ratio must be at least 100% at all times.

The Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) requires available stable funding to exceed required stable funding over a one-year horizon. The minimum NSFR is 100% and is reported quarterly to the supervisor.

Management must run liquidity stress tests monthly, covering idiosyncratic, market-wide and combined scenarios. Results and contingency funding actions must be reported to the board risk committee.
