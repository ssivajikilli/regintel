Security incidents are classified as low, medium, high or critical based on data sensitivity and business impact. Critical incidents must be escalated to the chief information security officer within one hour of detection.

Incidents involving personal data must be notified to the data protection authority within 72 hours of the organisation becoming aware of the breach, unless the breach is unlikely to result in risk to individuals.

A blameless post-incident review must be completed within 14 days of closure. It must document root cause, timeline, impact and corrective actions, and the actions are tracked to completion by the risk function.
