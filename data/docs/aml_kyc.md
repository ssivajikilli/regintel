Standard customers undergo customer due diligence: identity verification against government-issued documents, beneficial ownership identification, and screening against sanctions lists at onboarding and daily thereafter.

High-risk customers, including politically exposed persons and customers from high-risk jurisdictions, require enhanced due diligence: senior management approval, verification of source of wealth and source of funds, and transaction monitoring reviewed at least quarterly.

Suspicious activity reports must be filed with the financial intelligence unit within 30 days of detection. Staff must not tell the customer that a report has been filed. Records must be retained for five years after the relationship ends.
