Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital must be maintained at a minimum of 4.5% of risk-weighted assets at all times. A capital conservation buffer of 2.5% applies on top of the minimum, bringing the effective CET1 requirement to 7.0%.

Institutions that breach the conservation buffer face automatic restrictions on dividends, share buybacks and discretionary bonuses until the buffer is rebuilt. Management must submit a capital restoration plan to the supervisor within 30 days.

The leverage ratio, defined as Tier 1 capital divided by total exposure, must not fall below 3%. Systemically important institutions are subject to an additional surcharge.
