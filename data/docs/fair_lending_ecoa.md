Lenders must not discriminate in any aspect of a credit transaction on the basis of race, colour, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, receipt of public assistance income, or the good-faith exercise of consumer protection rights.

When credit is denied or offered on less favourable terms, an adverse action notice must be sent to the applicant within 30 days of receiving a completed application. The notice must state the specific principal reasons for the decision, not generic statements.

Automated underwriting models must be tested for disparate impact before release and at least annually. Where a model drives adverse action, the institution must be able to produce explainable reason codes for every individual decision.
