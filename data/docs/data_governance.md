Every dataset used for analytics or AI must have a named data owner, a documented source, and a classification of public, internal, confidential or restricted. Lineage must be recorded from source system to the final model or report.

Restricted data, including personal and financial identifiers, must be encrypted at rest and in transit and may only be used for the purpose for which it was collected. Personal data must be deleted or anonymised once its retention period ends.

Data quality is monitored through automated checks for completeness, validity and timeliness. Quality scores below 95% on critical datasets must be escalated to the data owner and resolved within ten business days.
