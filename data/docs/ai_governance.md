Every production AI model must be registered in the model inventory with an owner, intended use, risk tier, and a model card describing training data, limitations and evaluation results.

High-risk models, including those influencing credit decisions or claims handling, require independent validation before release and annual revalidation. Decisions must be explainable, for example with feature-attribution reports, and a human must be able to override them.

Generative AI systems must be grounded in approved sources, must cite those sources in responses, and must route low-confidence or policy-sensitive answers to a human reviewer. Prompt-injection testing is required before every release.
