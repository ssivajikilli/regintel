Every financed project must be screened for environmental and social risk before approval and assigned a category: Category A for projects with potentially significant adverse impacts, Category B for limited and reversible impacts, and Category C for minimal or no impact.

Category A projects require a full environmental and social impact assessment, public consultation with affected communities, and a management plan with monitoring indicators before financing is approved.

Projects involving involuntary resettlement must prepare a resettlement action plan guaranteeing compensation at replacement cost. A grievance mechanism must be available to affected people throughout the project life.
