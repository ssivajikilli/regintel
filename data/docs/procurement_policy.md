Procurement of goods and services must follow principles of value for money, transparency, fairness and economy. Contracts above USD 5 million require international competitive bidding with public advertisement of the tender.

Contracts between USD 500,000 and USD 5 million require national competitive bidding with at least three written quotations. Direct selection is permitted only in documented exceptional cases and requires senior management approval.

Bidders must disclose conflicts of interest. Evaluation committees must score proposals against criteria published in advance, and the full evaluation record must be retained for seven years for audit.
