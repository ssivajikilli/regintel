Before engaging a vendor that handles confidential data or supports a critical service, the institution must complete a risk assessment covering security controls, financial stability, business continuity and regulatory compliance.

Contracts with critical vendors must include audit rights, breach notification within 48 hours, subcontractor transparency, data return and deletion on termination, and an exit plan that has been tested.

Vendors providing AI or machine learning services require additional review of model documentation, training data provenance and any use of customer data to train models. Critical vendors are reassessed annually.
