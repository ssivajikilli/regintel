Lending decisions to sovereign borrowers must be informed by a debt sustainability analysis covering public debt levels, debt service burden, external financing needs and vulnerability to shocks.

Borrowers are assigned a debt distress risk rating of low, moderate, high or in debt distress. Countries rated high or in debt distress are limited to grant or highly concessional financing.

Debt data transparency is a condition of lending: borrowers must publish information on loan terms and report all new external borrowing to the institution at least quarterly.
